# 2026-autumn

T2TRG Online Interim Meeting

Constrained devices can be managed through YANG data models with
CORECONF, while applications reason about the same devices through
ontologies such as SOSA and SAREF.
Can the two be bridged systematically, and can we show that no
information is lost on the way?

AI agents and autonomous systems also need to know more than what a
device or service can do: is the network around it healthy and
trustworthy enough to act on?
Could resilience measurements and trust signals become information
that protocols expose and agents consume?

In this interim meeting on October 28th, we look at both,
and at further contributions listed in the agenda below.
